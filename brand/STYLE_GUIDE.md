# Burrows NC119 — Graphic Standard

**This document plus `00_REFERENCE_standard.png` define the look. Everything the campaign
publishes as a topic graphic should be recognisably the same object as that reference.**

---

## THE PALETTE — sampled directly from the approved reference

| Role | Hex | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Navy field | `#021528` | The entire background. Flat, unmodulated, no gradient. |
| Off-white | `#E9E8E6` | Display headline and the first part of the quote. |
| Sky | `#7CC3E7` | **One** phrase of the quote — the payoff line. Nowhere else. |
| Signal red | `#C21D22` | The horizontal rule, and the short bar above the attribution. Nowhere else. |

Four colours. There is no fifth. If a design needs another colour, the design is wrong.

## THE STRUCTURE

The reference is **1092 × 1440** (roughly 3:4). Anything from 4:5 to 3:4 holds together.

Top to bottom:

1. **Display headline** — two words, stacked, all caps, in a **heavy condensed sans**. Enormous:
   the cap height of each line is roughly a fifth of the canvas. It runs from the left margin and the
   second line is allowed to run behind the subject.
2. **Logo lockup** — top right, over the photograph. Mountain mark, MARK BURROWS, NC HOUSE 119.
3. **Full-width red rule** — around 47% down the canvas. It runs edge to edge and passes behind the subject.
4. **The quote** — left column, below the rule. Bold sans, tight leading, two sentences.
   The second sentence is sky blue. This is the only colour change in the type.
5. **Attribution** — bottom left. A short red bar, then `MARK BURROWS` in bold caps, then
   `30 YEARS OF PUBLIC SERVICE` and `NC HOUSE 119` in small letter-spaced caps.
6. **The photograph** — right side, full height, cut out of its original background and placed on the
   navy. He occupies roughly the right half and crops off the bottom edge. Hard edge, no glow,
   no drop shadow, no feathering.

## THE RULES THAT MAKE IT WORK

- **Two-word headlines only.** The whole effect depends on the display type being enormous. A third
  word forces it smaller and the design collapses. Find the two-word version of any topic first.
- **The quote is always Mark's own words.** Sourced from his recorded footage or his own published
  posts. Never a paraphrase, never something written for him.
- **One highlighted phrase.** The sky-blue line is the payoff. Two highlights is none.
- **He is large.** He should feel close and life-size, cropping at the frame edge — not floating small
  and centred with air around him.
- **Never alter his face.** Composite the photograph exactly as shot. No smoothing, no retouching,
  no regenerating. A synthesised likeness of a real candidate on a real ballot is a serious problem.
- **No other text.** No website, no date, no hashtag, no call to action, no sources on the card.

## THE HAT

He wears it constantly, so it belongs in the library — but it changes what the graphic says.

- **Hat on** (`MB-13`, `MB-12`, `MB-02`) — for content about *being out in the district*: recovery,
  canvassing, showing up, local presence.
- **Hat off** (`MB-04`, `MB-05`, `MB-06`, `MB-09`, `MB-10`) — for content about *competence in Raleigh*:
  budgets, policy, records, accountability.
- **Never** `MB-03`. Hat with a blazer reads as costume. It is in the folder only so nobody picks it by accident.

## THE THINGS THAT MAKE IT LOOK AI-GENERATED

Name these explicitly in every prompt, because the failure mode is always the same: **softness.**

No glow, bloom, lens flare or bokeh. No gradients behind the type. No glassmorphism or floating
rounded cards. No symmetrical centred composition. No faux-3D or bevels. No drop shadows on text.
No decorative flourishes. No second typeface. No invented text of any kind.
